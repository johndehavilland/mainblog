I don't know if you have been following the 2026 World Humanoid Robot Games - an olympics for robots - but it is pretty impressive. Its also pretty scary.

On one hand, machines outperform humans all the time (just look at the automobile). On the other hand, in this form factor, some of this is super impressive. As the tech evolves and AI continues to get better and better at world modelling, I see more and more autonomous robots in our future.
